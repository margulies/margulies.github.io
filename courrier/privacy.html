Privacy policy

Courrier is a personal application with one user, its author, Daniel Margulies. This page says what it does with Google data.

Last updated 12 October 2026.

What it accesses

With permissions granted on Google's consent screen: Gmail, to read and organise mail (gmail.modify) and, separately, to send mail (gmail.send); Google Calendar, to read calendars and add or change its user's own events (calendar.readonly, calendar.events). It never invites other people.

How it uses it

To show mail and calendar to its user, sort and summarise mail, suggest todos and replies, and send a message he confirmed. Message text is sent to Anthropic's Claude, through its user's own Claude account, for sorting, summaries and drafts; under that account's terms it is not used to train models. Google data is not used to develop, improve or train any machine-learning model.

Where it is kept

On the computer that runs Courrier, in a folder its user owns, with backups on the same computer. There is no server elsewhere.

Who it is shared with

No one. It is not sold, not shared and not used for advertising. Besides Google, only Anthropic's Claude receives it, as above.

Limited Use

Courrier's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

How to stop

Deleting Courrier's folder deletes everything it holds. Google's access can be withdrawn at any time under third-party access in the Google account.
